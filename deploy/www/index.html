akdbx relay

Encrypted pass-through for akdbx brokers.

This relay lets you open your akdbx broker's private pages from another device without an open port or a VPN. It forwards encrypted TLS connections only. Each broker keeps its own certificate key, so the relay cannot read your master password or any other value.

Addresses such as https://<id>.akdbx.link belong to individual brokers. There is nothing to sign up for here: run akdbx relay enable on your broker.

More secure options exist: a private proxy such as Tailscale Serve keeps the page reachable only from your own devices.
